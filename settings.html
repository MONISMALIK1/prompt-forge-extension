How the LLM enhancement works
When you click Enhance, Prompt Forge sends your rough prompt to the selected Ollama model with a system prompt that instructs it to think specifically about what YOU are building — not to paste generic best practices from a template.

If Ollama is not running, the extension automatically falls back to the built-in rule-based enhancer so you are never left without output.
